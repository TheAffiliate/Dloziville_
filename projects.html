Traditional Healthcare - Intando Yemvelo

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote a healthier more holistic lifestyle through the consumption of organic food and the use of natural remedies for ailments particularly in rural and peri-urban areas.

Our Vision

Our vision is to establish a facility where we will research and test African medical methods and treatments that align with the African laws of nature.

Community Wealth - Dloziville Stokvel

Our Mission

Our mission is to build a community that is financially responsible and is able to access funds that allow for an improved standard of living.

Our Vision

Our vision is to establish a traditional bank where community members will be able to acquire loans, assets and premium healthcare through their membership and contribution.
